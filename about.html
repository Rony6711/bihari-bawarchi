About Us

Brand introduction

Bihari Bawarchi is an Indian food brand created to bring authentic flavours inspired by culinary heritage into modern homes. Our first product is Shahi Treats. More sweets, snacks, specials and gift packs will follow.

Mission

To bring authentic and memorable Indian flavours to homes through thoughtfully crafted food products.

Vision

To build Bihari Bawarchi into a trusted Indian food brand known for quality, authenticity and memorable taste.

Values

Authenticity

Recipes and presentations rooted in Indian culinary tradition.

Care

Attention to taste, packing and the experience of sharing food.

Trust

Clear information, honest claims and quality-focused making.

Product philosophy

Every Bihari Bawarchi product should feel familiar yet contemporary — a pack you are proud to gift, and a flavour that reminds you of home. We only publish product facts provided by the company.
