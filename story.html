Our Story

From a love of traditional kitchens to a modern Indian food brand.

Where It Began

A kitchen, a memory, a name

Bihari Bawarchi started with the idea that the flavours of home should travel easily — from family celebrations to the modern grocery basket. The brand name honours the cook, the bawarchi, who turns simple ingredients into something people remember.

Our Inspiration

Indian culinary heritage

We draw from Bihar and the wider Indian table: festive sweets, thoughtful packing and the ritual of sharing food. Madhubani-inspired motifs appear as quiet accents, never as costume. The feeling we want is authentic, traditional, premium and trustworthy.

Shahi Treats is the first product of Bihari Bawarchi. It is a premium treat created for gifting, celebrations and everyday indulgence. Customers discover it here, then purchase on Amazon, Flipkart or Meesho.
